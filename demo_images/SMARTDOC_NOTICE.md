# Real retry demonstration attribution

`retry_capture_document.jpg` is derived from frame 85 (zero-based) of `datasheet001.avi`, SmartDoc 2015 Challenge 1 sample dataset, by Joseph Chazalon and Marçal Rusiñol. Original video decoded to PNG and converted to metadata-free JPEG (quality 95); no artificial blur applied. The frame visibly contains motion blur.
