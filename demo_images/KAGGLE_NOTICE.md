## Scanned Images Dataset for OCR and VLM finetuning

A Comprehensive Dataset for Advancing OCR Accuracy and VLM finetuning

### Categories and Characteristics

Advertisements: Images of promotional materials featuring mixed text, graphics, and unconventional layouts.

Emails: Scanned correspondence, including structured headers, signatures, and body content.

Forms: Predefined templates with structured data fields, such as application forms or invoices.

Letters: Personal or formal correspondence, showcasing handwritten and printed styles.

Memos: Internal business communications with concise text and headers.

News: Scanned newspaper clippings and articles, combining columns, images, and headlines.

Notes: Informal handwritten or typed notes, often featuring irregular spacing and formatting.

Reports: Documents with structured sections, tables, and graphs, such as financial or project reports.

Resumes: Personal profiles with a mix of structured text fields, tabular data, and occasional creative designs.

Scientific Papers: Complex layouts featuring dense text, mathematical equations, tables, and diagrams.

### Applications

OCR Development: The dataset is ideal for training and evaluating OCR systems due to its variety of fonts, layouts, and quality levels (e.g., scanned, photocopied, or degraded documents).

Vision-Language Model Fine-tuning: By combining text and layout understanding, this dataset supports the fine-tuning of VLMs for tasks like document classification, information extraction, and question-answering.

### Features

Diversity: Captures a broad spectrum of document types to simulate real-world OCR challenges.

Complex Layouts: Includes multi-column texts, tables, and mixed media.

Textual Variation: Covers both handwritten and printed text in multiple languages (if multilingual versions are included).

Scanned Image Quality: Contains variations in resolution, noise, and artifacts to replicate practical scenarios.
